Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers ask before their first order.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Our MOQ is just 50 pieces per style. That lets studios, influencers and emerging brands launch without heavy inventory risk.
How long does sampling and production take?
Sampling takes about 15 days; bulk production runs 15–25 days after sample approval. Express options are available worldwide.
Can you do fully custom fabrics, colors and logos?
Yes. We offer OEM/ODM: Pantone color matching, custom dye lots, printing & embroidery, woven labels, hang tags and branded packaging.
Which certifications do you hold?
We are OEKO-TEX, BSCI, GRS and REACH compliant, and can provide compliance documentation for your target markets.
